What I need to own

I should have listened to understand, not listened to defend myself.

Baebo, I am truly sorry for making you feel wrong when you were trying to tell me that something had hurt you. Instead of pausing, listening, and acknowledging your feelings, I became defensive. I shifted the blame and focused on explaining myself.

That was unfair to you. Even if I had my own perspective, it did not cancel out your experience. You deserved to feel heard and understood—not to have to prove why you were hurt.

I also know I haven't given you the time, attention, and emotional support you needed from me. I can imagine how lonely and disappointing that may have felt, especially when you were expecting me to be someone you could lean on.

I am sorry not only for the moments that hurt you, but also for the way I responded when you tried to tell me about that hurt.

About the trust I may have damaged

I understand that “trust me” is not enough.

I don't want to assume that everything is okay just because I have apologised. I understand that my actions may have made you question whether I will really show up for you, make time for you, and take your feelings seriously.

I cannot ask you to forget what happened, rush your feelings, or trust me just because I want things to be okay again. If trust has been shaken, I have to accept that—and do the patient work of earning it back.

And I don't want to make promises just to make this moment easier. I want to become more thoughtful in the ordinary moments too: listening without interrupting, making time instead of leaving you to ask, checking in, and being emotionally present.

You shouldn't have to fight to be heard by someone who says he cares about you. You shouldn't have to keep asking for time, reassurance, or emotional support. I am sorry that my actions made you feel otherwise.

I love the place you hold in my life, but I know saying that means very little if my actions don't make you feel valued. I want to do better—not for a day, not only while things feel difficult, but consistently.

You don't owe me an immediate reply, forgiveness, or reassurance. Take the time you need. If and when you're ready, I want to listen properly, without interrupting, arguing, or turning it back on you. I want to understand what this has been like for you.

I'm truly sorry, Baebo. You deserved more care from me, and I want my actions to show you that I understand.

With love, and with accountability. ❤️
